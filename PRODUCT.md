# PRODUCT.md — Orian

## Product Purpose
**Orian** is a 100% offline, native Windows desktop (`.exe`) study backlog prevention, exam triage, and focus recovery workbench built for students navigating messy real-world semesters.

## Register
`product` — Native desktop productivity workbench focused on clarity, calm urgency, honest math, and decisive action.

## Target Users
- University and college students juggling 3–7 simultaneous courses whose schedules shift constantly and whose workload spikes sharply around Class Tests (CTs), Quizzes, Midterms, and Finals.

## Real-World Academic Reality Orian Solves
1. **Imminent Exam & Class Test Triage (e.g., CT in 3 Days):**
   - When a student has a Class Test in 3 days covering Lectures 8–12 of `MATH204`, Orian does NOT blindly treat every course equally. It isolates the exact unwatched **in-syllabus lectures**, adds dedicated **exam revision blocks**, and smoothly shifts today's effort budget toward `MATH204` while deferring unrelated low-urgency backlog.
2. **Colliding Midterms & Finals Allocation:**
   - When two or three exams land in the same week, studying 100% for Tuesday's exam leaves Thursday's exam ruined. Orian computes **strain ratios** across all imminent exams and splits daily Pomodoro blocks proportionally (e.g., `4 blocks CS101 + 2 blocks PHYS112`).
3. **Exam Crunch Protection (Shield Deferral):**
   - When a Midterm/Final is `<= 48h` away (or an unprepped CT is `<= 24h` away), unrelated today's live classes are gracefully given Crunch Deferral so tonight's study hours aren't hijacked away from tomorrow's exam—and safely return to the post-exam recovery queue afterward.
4. **Manual Course Effort Shifting & Finals Mode:**
   - Students can boost any struggling course (`0.5x Light` to `2.0x Heavy Focus`) with optional auto-expiry, or flip **Finals Prep Mode** when semester lectures finish to devote 100% of daily study blocks to finals backlog and practice.
5. **Sudden Routine Chaos:**
   - Handles sudden online/recorded class switches (with an Online/Recorded Procrastination Guard), surprise off-day makeup classes, cancelled/rescheduled lectures, post-midnight study (`4:00 AM` Academic Day Cutoff), and multi-day absences cleanly without breaking the recurring weekly timetable.

## Brand Identity & Tone
- **Name:** Orian
- **Voice:** Plain verbs, sentence case, honest capacity diagnostics, zero hustle-culture fluff.
  - Good: `"CT 2 (MATH204) in 3d · 2 syllabus lectures + 4 practice blocks"` · `"Crunch deficit: add +2 blocks/day"`
  - Bad: `"Crush your exams with hyper-intelligent synergy!"`

## Anti-References
1. **Generic SaaS Dashboards:** No purple gradients, neon dark themes, or 4 identical KPI boxes.
2. **Naive Equal-Weight Schedulers:** Never ignore upcoming exams or force unrelated shields the night before a Midterm.
3. **Brittle Static Timetables:** One-off cancellations, online switches, or makeup classes never mutate next week's recurring template.
